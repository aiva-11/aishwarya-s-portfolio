My introduction

I’m Aishwarya S, a final-year Electronics and Communication Engineering student at K. Ramakrishnan College of Engineering, Anna University, with a clear ambition: to grow into a skilled Full Stack Developer.

My journey began with curiosity about how technology solves everyday problems, and it has grown into a genuine passion for Java, web development and database design.
